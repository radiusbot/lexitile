Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the menu and tap Restore Purchases, or tap Restore purchase at the bottom of the plans screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In Lexitile, open the menu and tap Manage Subscription. Or go there directly:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lexitile.
  • Android: Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Lexitile.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. You keep access until that period ends.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly and weekly plans start with 7 days free for new subscribers. You won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends; otherwise the plan starts automatically at the price shown when you subscribed. The lifetime purchase is a single payment with no trial.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested from them:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund help

Does Lexitile need an internet connection?

No. Searching, definitions and favorites all work offline. Lexitile checks your plan with the App Store or Google Play whenever it can; offline, it keeps working for the rest of the current billing period.

Where are my favorites and search history?

Only on your device. They aren't backed up or synced, so they don't carry over to a new device, and deleting the app deletes them.
