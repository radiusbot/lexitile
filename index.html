Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Lexitile - Scrabble Dictionary ("the app") is built to work entirely on your device. This page explains, plainly, what that means for your data.

No data collected

The app works entirely offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party.

Local storage only

Your search history and favorites are stored on your device, in a local database, and never leave it. Nothing is synced to a server — including ours, since we don't run one.

No ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Purchases

Subscription payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) via their respective in-app purchase systems. We never see, collect, or store your payment details.

Your data, your control

Because everything lives only on your device, you are always in control of it. You can clear your search history or remove individual favorites at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its local data, including history and favorites, since no copy exists anywhere else.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app collects no data at all from any user, this applies equally regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will also be reflected in the in-app Privacy Policy sheet, reachable from the app's menu and from the subscription screen.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to radiusbot@gmail.com.
